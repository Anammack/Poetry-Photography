I wake up and I'm not in your bed
I don't know where I am when I first open my eyes
But it isn't where I want to be
This place looks strange to me.
It's familiar but
I haven't seen it in a while
I look around for landmarks to help
My mothers?
Last night wasn't a dream
You ended things
And here I am again with my body trembling
And I keep reaching over with my closed eyes
Trying to trace my fingers down your jaw line
But when my hand touches the cold sheets beside me
My heart fucking breaks again
You are gone
And not just to work
You are gone
and I can't hold you anymore
I have 3 dream catchers in my old room
But they surly didn't stop this nightmare
My old bed smells like stale cigarettes and booze
And my mouth tastes like the lie I fed my mom as to
Why I am here and not where you are
Depression is one hell of a drug
And I think I'm going to be high for a while
Depression is one hell of a drug
But not one you can overdose on
Depression is one hell of a drug
But if I came home i wouldn't touch it again.
Depression is swallowing me whole
No need to chew me up and spit me out
When I am half an inch tall and I fold right into myself
Depression took me by the hand the moment I tried to leave and whispered to me
"Don't worry. You never have to leave this spot again" "I will stay here with you forever and you will never be alone through a dark night again"
Depression said
Never leave your bed.
You do not need food but
You need sleep
Depression said
If you lay here long enough you will sink under the sheet and into the springs and no one will bother you anymore
Depression said you will be a pile of bones who can't stay warm but I will be your blanket
Depression said
I'll be here till the end.
