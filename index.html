Cold Sheets and Trembling Hands

I wake up and I'm not in your bed
I don't know where I am when I first open my eyes
But it isn't where I want to be
This place looks strange to me.
It's familiar but
I haven't seen it in a while
I look around for landmarks to help
My mothers?
Last night wasn't a dream
You ended things
And here I am again with my body trembling
And I keep reaching over with my closed eyes
Trying to trace my fingers down your jaw line
But when my hand touches the cold sheets beside me
My heart fucking breaks again
You are gone
And not just to work
You are gone
and I can't hold you anymore
I have 3 dream catchers in my old room
But they surly didn't stop this nightmare
My old bed smells like stale cigarettes and booze
And my mouth tastes like the lie I fed my mom as to
Why I am here and not where you are
Depression is one hell of a drug
And I think I'm going to be high for a while
Depression is one hell of a drug
But not one you can overdose on
Depression is one hell of a drug
But if I came home i wouldn't touch it again.
Depression is swallowing me whole
No need to chew me up and spit me out
When I am half an inch tall and I fold right into myself
Depression took me by the hand the moment I tried to leave and whispered to me
"Don't worry. You never have to leave this spot again" "I will stay here with you forever and you will never be alone through a dark night again"
Depression said
Never leave your bed.
You do not need food but
You need sleep
Depression said
If you lay here long enough you will sink under the sheet and into the springs and no one will bother you anymore
Depression said you will be a pile of bones who can't stay warm but I will be your blanket
Depression said
I'll be here till the end.

Unmarked Grave

I love you so fucking much
But you can’t love someone back to life
Your body is earth again
And mine is forced to keep growing
To keep going
Every sunrise and sunset that you paint in my sky is breathtaking
And All those times you made it rain
Why can’t you make your lungs breathe again
Why can’t you be here with me again
To share these same laughs and bad habits
I can’t write beautiful things about you because there is nothing beautiful about this
There is nothing beautiful about the way grief digs its thorns deep into my dna and rips out pieces of me that I can’t ever get back again
Because they belong to you again
And you’re not coming back this time
You’re laid to rest in an unmarked grave that I struggle to find when I come to sit by your side
When my tears won’t stop flowing and my skin is blistered with salt stains and shaking bones
I beg the universe to give you back to me
But your body is earth again
And you lay in an unmarked grave
And I never stop searching for you
And I just want to become part of you again
I lay on the freshly grown back grass and beg the bugs to eat me whole
To bring me to you
To put me to rest
But it never happens and I pick myself up and dust off the very earth that your body has become
And I keep going
Because your body is earth again
And I love you so fucking much
But You can’t love someone back to life

Haunted Bodies

I used to say “I am so soft for you”
But I realize now what I meant to say was
“I’m easily manipulated by you”.
And when I finally built up the courage to say
“It’s easy telling you no”
I didn’t mean it
But what I did mean was
“I’m tired of letting you manipulate me”
And moments later I’ll crash into your arms again
Spill out another pathetic
“I love you”
Because I’ll always
“Be soft for you”
Because you are someone I’ll always want
And you know you can come and go as you please
My heart is a revolving door for you
The glowing “open” sign Left in the window of a boarded up house that never got turned off.
Forgotten about until one lonely night
you take a drive and pass by
Youll stop in.
Rip a few boards off of the doors.
Stay for a couple of hours and
always be gone by

Forever Girl

I admitted that I already care too much And you told me that I was right.
And I think that means this is going to hurt The same way it always has Every time a boy with bright eyes And sun kissed skin
Traces their fingers across my empty stomach And thighs that were made too small for you
The same words will be said
The way they always are
That I was fun for the moment but I'm not a forever girl
I have the blue hair and crazy ideas
I drink too much and have suicidal tendencies
But the moment things get real and the fun runs out And I have a bad day... It is no longer what you wanted.
I know this is going to hurt
I can tell by the calm in my stomach when you kiss me
By the plans I'm willing to cancel for you By my willingness to open up
~ the small girl who is easy to leave
